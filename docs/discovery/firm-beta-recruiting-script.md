# Firm Beta Recruiting Script

Purpose: recruit Canadian small CPA firms, bookkeepers, and tax preparers for discovery interviews and later privacy-safe pilots. The message must not imply TaxAgent files returns, replaces tax software, or gives autonomous tax advice.

## Target Firms

Best initial targets:

- Small Canadian CPA firms with 3 to 15 staff.
- Bookkeepers or tax preparers who prepare high volumes of T1 returns.
- Firms using TaxCycle, ProFile, Taxprep, Cantax, DT Max, QuickBooks, Excel, DoxCycle, Dext, TaxDome, Canopy, Karbon, or similar tools.
- Firms with visible T1, personal tax, student, young worker, rental, or self-employment return workflows.
- Firms open to technology but not necessarily looking to replace existing prep software.

Avoid for first outreach:

- Large enterprise firms with long procurement cycles.
- Firms focused mainly on T2, audit, assurance, or corporate tax.
- Firms that require full direct integration before conversation.
- Firms unwilling to discuss workflow without live filing guarantees.

## Short Email

Subject options:

- Research question on T1 intake workflow
- Quick question for your tax season workflow
- Canadian T1 intake and review research

Message:

```text
Hi [Name],

I am researching how Canadian small tax firms handle T1 intake, document collection, and review during tax season. I am trying to understand where the real bottlenecks are — before building more product.

The project is called TaxAgent. The idea is a T1 intake and review workbench that sits before TaxCycle, ProFile, Taxprep, Cantax, DT Max, or whatever you use: organize client documents, extract key fields, preserve source links, flag missing items, and produce a review-ready package. We are not trying to replace your filing workflow.

I am looking to speak with preparers, reviewers, and firm owners about how T1 intake, CRA Auto-fill, non-CRA documents, client chasing, and workpapers actually work in practice.

Would you be open to a 25-minute research call? No client data is needed, and I am not asking you to trial anything yet.

Thanks,
[Name]
TaxAgent
```

## Phone Or Live Intro Script

```text
We are building TaxAgent, but right now we are only doing discovery.

The hypothesis is that small firms do not need another tax filing engine. They need a better way to turn scattered client documents, non-CRA records, corrections, missing items, and source evidence into a review-ready package before the file reaches their existing tax software.

I would like to understand your workflow and where the real pain is. I will not ask for client data, and I am not asking you to change tools.
```

## Clear Ask

Ask for one of:

- 25-minute discovery call.
- Review of a prototype later.
- Introduction to the staff member who manages T1 intake.
- Permission to follow up after tax season.
- Interest in a privacy-safe pilot using anonymized or explicitly approved files.

## Privacy-Safe Pilot Language

Use this language when discussing pilots:

```text
A pilot would be designed around privacy and review control. We would not need SINs, and we would not file returns. Any real client files would require explicit firm/client approval or anonymization. The product would produce a review package for your staff; your firm would remain responsible for review, preparation, and filing in your existing software.
```

## What Not To Promise

Do not promise:

- CRA NETFILE filing.
- Replacement of TaxCycle, ProFile, Taxprep, Cantax, DT Max, QuickBooks, or existing portals.
- Fully automated tax preparation.
- AI tax advice.
- AI tax calculations.
- Direct software integrations in the first pilot.
- Error-free OCR.
- No reviewer involvement.
- Broad connectors.

## Qualification Questions

Use these before scheduling a pilot:

1. How many T1 files do you prepare annually?
2. Which tax software do you use?
3. Do you use CRA Auto-fill?
4. Which documents still arrive outside CRA Auto-fill?
5. Who handles intake and missing-item follow-up?
6. Who reviews source documents?
7. What would make a pilot useful to you?
8. Would you be comfortable using anonymized or approved files?
9. What privacy/security requirement would block a pilot?
10. What time of year is best for a low-risk test?

## Segmentation Tags

Track each lead with:

- Firm size.
- T1 annual volume.
- Software stack.
- CRA Auto-fill usage.
- Non-CRA document pain.
- Review/workpaper pain.
- Export preference.
- AI concern level.
- Pilot interest.
- Best follow-up date.
